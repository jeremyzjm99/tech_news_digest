OpenAI’s new reasoning technique alarms AI safety experts

OpenAI is introducing a new model called Astra that uses a technique called 'recurrent depth,' enabling it to think in a non-sequential way unlike most current reasoning models. This departure from standard reasoning approaches has raised concerns among AI safety experts, who worry about the unpredictability and potential risks of models that operate outside established thinking patterns. The development signals a significant shift in how large language models may process and reason through complex problems.

Fine-tuning a 350M Model for Better Structured Outputs in 100 GRPO Steps
