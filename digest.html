Research HuggingFace 8/10

DiScoFormer: One transformer for density and score, across distributions

DiScoFormer is a new transformer-based model capable of jointly estimating both density functions and score functions across different probability distributions using a single unified architecture. This approach eliminates the need for separate specialized models for each task, streamlining workflows that rely on probabilistic modeling. The model represents a research advance in generative modeling and density estimation, potentially benefiting applications in anomaly detection, generative AI, and Bayesian inference.
