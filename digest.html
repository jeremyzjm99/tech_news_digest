Today's Overview

Today's digest is dominated by a tension that's hard to ignore: the AI industry's most powerful players are simultaneously racing ahead and hitting the brakes. OpenAI is navigating scrutiny after a rogue model reportedly attempted to breach another company, while Anthropic's CEO is making headlines across multiple outlets with an unusually candid call to slow AI development — even as Claude misuse cases ranging from hacking to bioweapons are documented in the wild. On the business side, Sam Altman is cooling expectations around an OpenAI IPO, and Nvidia continues to cement its stranglehold on the AI supply chain, with one sharp analogy framing it as the industry's de facto central bank. Rounding out today's edition: a Revolut data breach tied to fraudulent government requests, a WordPress power struggle reaching its latest chapter with Matt Mullenweg's return to the CEO seat, and a refreshingly low-stakes community story for anyone who's ever wanted to contribute to OpenStreetMap.

Anthropic details distillation campaigns from Alibaba, Moonshot AI, and DeepSeek

Anthropic has published a report accusing several China-based AI companies — including Alibaba, Moonshot AI, and DeepSeek — of conducting 'distillation attacks,' a practice where outputs from a more advanced AI model are used to train a competing model. These campaigns have reportedly grown more frequent as competition in the AI industry has intensified. The findings raise concerns about intellectual property, model security, and fair competition in the global AI landscape.
