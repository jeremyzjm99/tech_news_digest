Tools HuggingFace 7/10

LFM2.5 Q4\_0 Checkpoints from Quantization-Aware Distillation

Liquid AI has released quantized checkpoints of their LFM2.5 model using a Quantization-Aware Distillation (QAD) process, resulting in Q4_0 format weights optimized for efficient deployment. This approach combines quantization and knowledge distillation during training to minimize accuracy loss compared to standard post-training quantization. The release makes the model more accessible for inference on resource-constrained hardware.
