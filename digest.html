Pruning LLMs Like a Physicist: Block Removal as an Ising Optimization Problem

Researchers propose a novel method for pruning large language models (LLMs) by framing the removal of transformer blocks as an Ising optimization problem, a concept borrowed from physics. This approach systematically identifies and removes redundant layers to reduce model size while preserving performance. The technique offers a more principled alternative to ad-hoc pruning strategies, potentially enabling more efficient LLM deployment.

OpenAI forms math advisory group as its AI resolves more than 100 open problems

OpenAI has established a mathematics advisory group following its AI systems' success in solving over 100 previously unsolved mathematical problems. However, the advisory group will have a limited role, with no authority to slow down or redirect OpenAI's existing math research efforts. This move signals OpenAI's growing focus on mathematical reasoning as a benchmark for AI capability.

Policy The Verge 7/10

California tightens rules on AI data center energy and water use

California Governor Gavin Newsom has signed seven bills regulating how AI data centers consume energy and water, with a key focus on preventing data centers from shifting utility upgrade costs to residents. The legislation directs the California Public Utilities Commission to create a new rate classification specifically for data centers and requires the facilities to fund their own infrastructure improvements. These measures reflect growing concerns about the environmental and financial strain that rapid AI infrastructure expansion places on public utilities.
