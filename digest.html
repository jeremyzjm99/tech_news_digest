Today's Overview

Anthropic details distillation campaigns from Alibaba, Moonshot AI, and DeepSeek

Anthropic has published a report accusing several China-based AI companies — including Alibaba, Moonshot AI, and DeepSeek — of conducting 'distillation attacks,' a practice where outputs from a more advanced AI model are used to train a competing model. Meta is facing a proposed class action lawsuit alleging it illegally harvested photos from Facebook and Instagram to train its AI image-generation models without user consent.
