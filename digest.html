AI Could Help Fossil Fuel Companies Create More Emissions

New research warns that AI's integration into the fossil fuel industry could boost operational efficiency in ways that increase carbon emissions by up to 5 percent — a figure that far exceeds the emissions generated by AI data centers themselves. The findings challenge the common framing of AI's environmental footprint as primarily a compute/energy problem, highlighting how productivity gains in high-emission industries may be the larger risk. This presents a complex tradeoff for data professionals building AI solutions for energy sector clients.
