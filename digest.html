Research VentureBeat AI 8/10

Agentic orchestration: Enterprise AI organizations have a deployment problem, not a platform problem — and most are calling chatbots agents

A June 2026 survey of 101 enterprises found that while organizations are rapidly consolidating AI agent orchestration onto model-provider platforms—led by Anthropic's Claude at 40%—most deployments remain basic chatbot wrappers rather than true multi-step agents, with 71% of respondents reporting that a quarter or fewer of their 'agents' perform genuine orchestrated workflows. Enterprises prioritize reliable multi-step execution as their success metric but lag significantly in operational maturity, with over a quarter lacking any real-time mechanism to halt runaway token spend. The majority plan a hybrid control architecture by end of 2026 to mitigate vendor lock-in, while investment flows primarily into workflow tooling and security/permissions enforcement to close the gap between orchestration ambition and deployed reality.
