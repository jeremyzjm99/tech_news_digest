Why your local LLM feels dumber than it is

Local LLMs often appear less capable than they actually are, not due to model quality but because of suboptimal configuration and inference settings that users apply by default. Factors like quantization, context window settings, system prompts, and sampling parameters can significantly degrade output quality when not properly tuned. Getting the most out of a locally-run model requires deliberate setup that cloud-hosted APIs typically handle automatically behind the scenes.
