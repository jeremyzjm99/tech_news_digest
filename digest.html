Anthropic details distillation campaigns from Alibaba, Moonshot AI, and DeepSeek

Anthropic has published a report accusing several China-based AI companies — including Alibaba, Moonshot AI, and DeepSeek — of conducting 'distillation attacks,' a practice where outputs from a more advanced AI model are used to train a competing model.
