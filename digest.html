Apertus – Open Foundation Model for Sovereign AI

Apertus is an open foundation model initiative focused on enabling 'Sovereign AI,' allowing organizations or nations to run and control their own AI systems independently. The project aims to provide a transparent, open alternative to proprietary large language models, reducing reliance on major commercial AI providers. It is positioned as a resource for entities that prioritize data privacy, autonomy, and self-hosted AI infrastructure.
