Policy The Verge 8/10

Europe’s AI labeling and transparency rules are now in effect

The EU's AI Act transparency rules took effect on August 2nd, requiring companies to clearly disclose when users are interacting with AI systems such as chatbots, and to label AI-generated content like deepfakes. These obligations are part of the broader EU AI Act, the bloc's landmark regulatory framework for artificial intelligence. The rules aim to increase accountability and help consumers distinguish between human and AI-generated interactions and content.

Industry The Verge 8/10

AMD’s datacenter business is booming while gaming takes a backseat

AMD's data center revenue more than doubled year-over-year, reaching $6.7 billion in the latest quarter, driven largely by strong demand for AI infrastructure. This represents a 107% increase compared to the same period last year and a sequential rise from $5.8 billion in Q1. However, AMD's gaming segment saw a significant decline, with revenue falling 31%, highlighting a clear shift in the company's growth drivers toward AI and enterprise computing.
