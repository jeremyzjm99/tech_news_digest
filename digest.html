OpenAI has unveiled its first custom AI chip, developed in partnership with Broadcom. The approach raises both opportunities for automation and questions about oversight and control.

Nearly Half of LG Smart TV Apps Contain Residential Proxy SDKs

A significant portion of apps available on LG Smart TVs have been found to contain residential proxy SDKs, which can route third-party internet traffic through users' devices without their knowledge. This type of software turns consumer devices into nodes in a proxy network, raising serious privacy and security concerns. The finding highlights hidden data practices embedded in widely used consumer applications.
