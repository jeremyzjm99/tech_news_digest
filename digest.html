Research Hacker News 9/10

DSpark: Speculative decoding accelerates LLM inference [pdf]

DSpark introduces a speculative decoding technique to speed up inference in large language models (LLMs). The approach uses a smaller draft model to predict multiple tokens at once, which a larger model then verifies in parallel, reducing the number of sequential steps required. This can significantly lower latency without sacrificing output quality.
