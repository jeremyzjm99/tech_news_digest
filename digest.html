Quantization-Aware Healing: a compressed, 4-bit model that outperforms its full-precision original

HuggingFace researchers introduced a technique called Quantization-Aware Healing, which allows a model compressed to 4-bit precision to outperform its original full-precision version. This approach addresses the typical quality loss associated with quantization by incorporating a healing step that recovers and even improves model performance. The findings suggest that aggressive compression does not have to come at the cost of accuracy, which has significant implications for deploying models on resource-constrained hardware.
