Fine-tuning a 350M Model for Better Structured Outputs in 100 GRPO Steps

A HuggingFace article demonstrates how a relatively small 350 million parameter language model can be fine-tuned to produce better structured outputs using a reinforcement learning technique called GRPO, achieved in just 100 training steps. The approach shows that structured output reliability can be significantly improved without large-scale compute resources. This highlights the growing accessibility of fine-tuning techniques for teams working with smaller, more efficient models.
