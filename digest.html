Google Has Removed MV2 Extensions from the Chrome Web Store, Including UBO

Google has removed Manifest V2 (MV2) browser extensions from the Chrome Web Store, including the widely used ad blocker uBlock Origin (UBO). This is part of Google's planned transition to Manifest V3 (MV3), a new extension framework that critics argue limits the capabilities of ad blockers and privacy tools. The move affects millions of users who relied on MV2-based extensions for content filtering and ad blocking.
