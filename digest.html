What We Learned by Reproducing 2,200 papers from ICML

HuggingFace attempted to reproduce over 2,200 machine learning papers from ICML, uncovering widespread reproducibility challenges in published research. The effort highlighted how often key details such as code, data, and hyperparameters are missing or insufficient to replicate reported results. The findings underscore a systemic reproducibility crisis in ML research and call for stronger transparency standards in academic publishing.
